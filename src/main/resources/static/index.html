Show you APRS friends some love when they are nearby!

APRS Welcome Center allows you to reach out to those around you and make APRS more informative and exciting. Put those GPS beacons to work!

To get started:

  1. Create a welcome center and define the geographical area it handles.
  2. Create a communication plan to determine what information is sent to those entering and exiting that geographical area.

Providing useful and smart information is a great way for you to maximize the impact of APRS!
